      About the data

      A work-in-progress comparison of Brno’s 48 cadastral areas. It helps explore trade-offs, rather than define one best place to live.

      Where the data comes from

      Cadastral boundaries, trees, parks, cycling routes and the original amenity layers come from Brno’s official publicly accessible data portal. Additional learning, grocery, culture and fitness locations, plus the walking network, come from OpenStreetMap. These are saved datasets, not live listings; their dates and completeness vary.

      Walking access

      Coverage estimates the share of land area within walking distance of at least one destination—not the share of residents. It uses 25 m samples and paths in the supplied network.

      • Transit and social services: 400 m
      • Cafés/restaurants and groceries: 500 m
      • Learning and fitness/wellness: 800 m
      • Culture: 1,000 m
      • Education: kindergarten 800 m, school/college 1,500 m, university 2,000 m

      Connections to paths can be up to 100 m and count toward distance. They may cross unmapped obstacles. Steps are included; this is not wheelchair routing. Opening hours, capacity and entrances are not verified. Address/pin results show the nearest two reachable destinations per category, within a 5 km search limit.

      How scores work

      Eleven factors start equally weighted. “Your Priority” changes their contribution to the overall score; missing scores are omitted. Trees, parks and cycling retain their original density or area measures.

      Education combines kindergarten (50%), school/college (35%) and university (15%) coverage. Social-service coverage is scaled against the highest-coverage area. These formulas were inferred from the original project and retained for this prototype.

      Fitness and school proximity

      Fitness locations within 100 m of the existing school points are excluded. All other supplied fitness records are retained, regardless of access tags. This approximates school overlap and does not confirm public access.

      Relative rental cost

      August 2026 snapshot from the Czech Ministry of Finance, using data through June 2026. Figures are modeled reference rents for existing two-room apartments (2+kk / 2+1), in Kč/m²/month, excluding utilities and service charges—not average asking rents.

      Cost bands compare each area with the median of 48 area estimates. They are project-defined and remain separate from overall livability scores. Small differences may fall within model uncertainty.

      Overall Livability Score

      An average of eleven factors weighted by your priorities. All factors start equally weighted; missing scores are omitted. Access to services is measured along the walking network.

      How walking coverage is estimated

      Coverage is estimated across land area using 25 m samples and routes along streets and paths. Connections to the nearest path, up to 100 m, count toward the walking distance. Locations farther from a path count as unserved. Connections may cross unmapped obstacles, and entrances have not been verified. Steps are included.

      Tree density, park coverage and cycling density retain their original measurements. Education categories with no coverage receive zero.
